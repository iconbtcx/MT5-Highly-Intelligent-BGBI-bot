BGBI V3.5.1 — STOP-GEOMETRY PATCH / TRADE REVIEW

Scope
- Based on the supplied BGBI-SUPER-PERFECT-CORRECTED V3.5 package.
- One source-level calculation is corrected: SlippagePoints is no longer included in ComputeSLDistance().
- SlippagePoints remains available for CTrade/OrderSend execution deviation.
- New stop distances continue to use ATR, spread, broker stop-level and safety-floor logic.
- All ten active instrument presets are retained unchanged. XAUUSD remains optional and outside the target universe.

Why this correction was made
- The previous formula was (CurrentSpreadPoints + SlippagePoints) * _Point * SL_SpreadMultiplier.
- Presets used SlippagePoints=200 and SL_SpreadMultiplier=2.0.
- On 5-digit FX quotes this mechanically added about 40 pips before the spread component.
- The supplied open-position screenshot showed approximately 41.6–42.3 pip stops on EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDCAD and USDJPY, consistent with this calculation.

Visible history sample supplied 2026-11-05
- 12 visible closed trades: gross wins +9.28; gross losses -40.70; net -31.42; PF approximately 0.228; 4 wins and 8 losses.
- Three visible DE40 losses total -30.04. Small sample; not a long-run performance estimate.
- Separate live-position screenshot showed floating P/L -12.70 and an XAUUSD position.

Important controls not changed in this patch
- Kronos remains Mode 1 advisory (bounded score contribution); no direct SL/TP or lot-size authority.
- Profit ratchet settings and TP_RR_Swing=3.50 remain unchanged to keep this a single-variable patch.
- The configured account-wide risk governor counts open positions and pending orders sharing the same MagicNumber across symbols. An out-of-universe XAUUSD position can therefore consume portfolio risk budget.

Validation status
- Source text replacement and archive integrity are checked.
- MetaEditor compilation, Strategy Tester validation, historical forecast replay and demo execution have NOT been performed here.
- This is a DEMO-ONLY candidate. Do not replace an EA on a chart that is managing an open position without first arranging safe position management.
- Existing positions and pending orders retain their already-placed SL/TP; this source correction affects newly calculated orders after the patched EA is compiled and attached.
